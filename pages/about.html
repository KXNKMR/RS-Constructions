WHO WE ARE

Turning Construction Requirements Into Real-World Spaces.

RS Enterprises is a construction company based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

Our work spans a range of construction and specialised service requirements, including building construction, civil and structural works, concrete and epoxy flooring, waterproofing, road works, infrastructure, interior execution and renovation.

We recognise that every project comes with its own purpose, requirements and practical considerations. Understanding these needs is an important part of developing the right approach to each project.

From initial planning considerations to the different stages of construction, our focus is on bringing the requirements of a project together through practical solutions and considered execution.
